Audio — Track

Step 1

Key title

Optional label shown at the top of the LCD key. Leave empty to use Audio — Track.
Step 2

Audio track

Choose one OBS audio input. Freeze belongs to this selected track and keeps its highest peak until reset.
Step 3

Display mode

Live + Peak shows both values. Absolute modes compare the peak with your preferred range.
Step 4

Good range

The peak is OK while it stays between these dBFS limits. Typical starting point: -20 to -8.
